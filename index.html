Odomark – servisní knížka

Servis a provozní náklady vašeho vozidla. Záznamy zůstávají ve vašem zařízení.

Co to je

Odomark je aplikace pro Android, ve které si vedete servisní historii a náklady na vozidlo: servis, tankování se spotřebou v l/100 km, pojištění, STK, pneumatiky a další úkony. Aplikace hlídá i termíny a stav tachometru a připomene, co se blíží.

Bez účtu, bez reklam, bez analytiky. Záznamy, vozidla, připomínky a fotky jsou uložené v zařízení (Android je může zálohovat do vašeho účtu Google). Nic se neodesílá, dokud to sami nespustíte (export, sdílení).

Aplikace se připravuje pro Android. Odkaz na Google Play přibude po vydání.

Odomark – car service log

Maintenance and running costs for your vehicle. Your records stay on your device.

What it is

Odomark is an Android app for keeping a service history and the costs of your vehicle: service, refuelling with consumption in l/100 km, insurance, inspection, tyres and other jobs. It also tracks due dates and odometer readings and reminds you of what is coming up.

No account, no ads, no analytics. Records, vehicles, reminders and photos are stored on the device (Android may back them up to your Google account). Nothing is sent anywhere until you trigger it yourself (export, share).

The app is being prepared for Android. A Google Play link will be added after release.

Free and Pro

Free

Pro – one-time 3.59 EUR

Pro is bought once through Google Play, not as a subscription. Google processes the payment. The price in your local currency is shown in Google Play.

Shared log

One file in your own Google Drive, and you sign in with Google to use it. You share it by e-mail with the other person, who joins with one tap in the app. The app sees only that file. Photos are not shared. Your data never passes through the developer, and there is no server. Details are in the privacy policy.
